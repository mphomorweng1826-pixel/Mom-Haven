Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mom's Haven an emergency service?

No. If you or your baby are in immediate danger, contact emergency services immediately.

Can a nurse diagnose me through the website?

Mom's Haven is designed to connect mothers with nurses for professional guidance. It does not replace an in-person medical assessment.

When should I take my baby for the first check-up?

Your baby should been seen within 3-5 days of birth, then again at 2 weeks. After that, baby visits are recommended at 1,2,4,6,9 and 12 months. These visits include track growth, immunisations and development of the child. Your Road to Health booklet records all of, keep it safe.

Can I choose how I communicate with the nurse?

Mothers can communicate with a nurse through chat, voice call or video call.

Is the normal to feel like I am not bonding with my baby?

Yes, and it is more common than many mothers admit. Bonding is not always instant — it can build gradually over days or weeks. However, if you feel persistently detached, numb, or unable to care for your baby, this may be a sign of postnatal depression. Please reach out to one of our nurses. You are not a bad mother, you are a mother who needs support.
