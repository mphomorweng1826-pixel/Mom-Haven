Why Mom’s Haven?

A safe space for every mother, whenever support is needed.

Created for the moments when support feels out of reach.

Mom's Haven is a support platform created to help new mothers find the right help when they need it. We understand that becoming a mother can come with many questions, concerns and unexpected situations, especially when professional support is not always easy to access.

Our goal is to make it easier for mothers to explain what they are experiencing and connect with an available nurse for professional guidance.

Our Purpose

We aim to make maternal support more accessible, simple and convenient, particularly for mothers who may live far from healthcare facilities or have difficulty accessing support outside normal healthcare hours. Mom's Haven is designed around the idea that a mother should not have to figure everything out alone when she is unsure about what to do.
